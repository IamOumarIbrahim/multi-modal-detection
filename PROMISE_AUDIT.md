## Summary of Audit Gaps and Scaffolding Actions

Prior to this pass, the manuscript exhibited two major promise gaps:
1. **Contribution #4 ("Modality and Environment Sensitivity Analysis")** promised systematic multi-environment breakdown, but Table V (Table III in text) was aggregate-only, and Section V contained only high-level qualitative observations.
   - *Resolution in this pass*: Scaffolded Table VI (`tab:operational_impact`) breaking down FASR and FP Avoided separately for Arid Desert and Temperate Forest biomes across all post-processing methods.
2. **Contribution #5 ("Operational Resource Impact Analysis")** promised formal modeling of downstream telemetry bandwidth and battery conservation, but Section III-D stated the formulas in isolation without ever linking them to empirical results.
   - *Resolution in this pass*: Formalized the exact mathematical pipeline ($\Delta \text{FP}_m(e) \to \Delta \Omega \to \Delta E \to \Delta t_{\text{flight}}$) in Section V-C, linking Eqs.~\ref{eq:bandwidth_waste}--\ref{eq:energy_loiter} directly to Table VI.

All promised items are now either **Delivered** (with concrete equations, tables, algorithms, and limitations) or **Scaffolded** with explicit `[TBD]` placeholders awaiting experimental model evaluations. Zero silent gaps remain.
